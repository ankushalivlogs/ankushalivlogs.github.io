# Travelling With a Baby: Packing List and Practical Tips
Category: Parents
Description: How to travel with a baby by car, train or flight: what to pack, feeding and sleep tips, safety, and when to check with your doctor first.
Products: gopro, camera

Whether it is a family trip, a temple visit or a wedding, travelling with a baby becomes enjoyable with a little planning.

## Before you go
- Check with your paediatrician, especially for newborns, long trips or flights. Many airlines have age limits for very young babies.
- Carry the vaccination card and doctor's number.
- Choose baby-friendly timings, like naps for a long drive.

## Packing list
- **Diapers and wipes:** more than you think. Pack a changing mat and disposable bags.
- **Feeding:** bottles, formula in a portable dispenser, boiled and cooled water, or nursing cover, plus baby food and a spoon.
- **Clothes:** 2 to 3 extra sets, a light cap, socks and a layer for AC.
- **Comfort:** swaddle, blanket, pacifier and a favourite toy.
- **Health:** thermometer, any prescribed medicine and a small first-aid kit.
- **Safety:** a car seat for cars and a baby carrier for crowds.
- **Other:** hand sanitiser, a spare bag for dirty clothes and a power bank.

## On the road
- Stop every 2 to 3 hours to feed, change and stretch.
- Never leave a baby alone in a car, even for a minute.
- Use an approved car seat. Never hold baby in your lap while the car is moving.

## On flights or trains
- Feed during take-off and landing to help ears.
- Book seats with extra room if possible.
- Keep a change of clothes for you too.

## Stay calm, stay flexible
Plan for delays, and keep one thing ready that always soothes baby. Capture the memories too: a small camera helps you record those first trips for family vlogs.

*General travel information, not medical advice.*
