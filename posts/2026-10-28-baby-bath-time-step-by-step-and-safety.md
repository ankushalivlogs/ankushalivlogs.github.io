# Baby Bath Time: Step-by-Step Guide and Safety Tips
Category: Babies
Description: How to bathe a baby safely: water temperature, what you need, step-by-step bathing, sponge baths for newborns and key safety rules.

Pehli baar baby ko nehlana dar jaisa lagta hai. Thodi si taiyari aur ye mazedaar routine ban jaata hai.

## Sponge bath first
Until the umbilical cord stump falls off and heals, give a **sponge bath** with a soft damp cloth instead of putting baby in a tub.

## Get everything ready
Towel, mild baby wash, soft cloth, clean diaper, clean clothes, and a small tub. Keep everything within arm's reach **before** you start.

## Water temperature
Aim for warm, around 37 to 38 degrees Celsius. Always test with your elbow or a bath thermometer. Fill the tub with only a few centimetres of water.

## Step by step
1. Undress baby and keep them wrapped in a towel until you are ready.
2. Lower baby in slowly, feet first, supporting the head and neck.
3. Wash gently from top to bottom: face with plain water first, then body, then skin folds and the diaper area last.
4. Use a cup to rinse. Avoid getting water in the face.
5. Lift baby out, wrap in the towel straight away, and pat dry, especially the folds.
6. Dress quickly, and moisturise if the skin is dry.

## Safety rules
- **Never leave a baby alone in the bath, even for a second.** If the phone rings, take baby with you.
- Check the water temperature every time.
- Set your water heater to a safe level so taps cannot scald.
- Keep baby wash mild, fragrance free and tear free.

## How often?
Newborns do not need a bath every day. Two or three times a week is usually enough, with face, neck and diaper area cleaned daily.
