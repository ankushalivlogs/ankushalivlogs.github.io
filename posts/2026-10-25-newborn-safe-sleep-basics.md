# Newborn Safe Sleep: The Basics Every New Parent Should Know
Category: Babies
Description: Safe sleep rules for newborns: back to sleep, firm flat surface, empty cot, room sharing and how to avoid overheating.

Naya baby, nayi neend ki tension. Ye rules yaad rakhna bahut zaroori hai kyunki ye sudden infant death (SIDS) ka risk kam karte hain.

## The ABCs of safe sleep
- **A**lone: baby sleeps in their own cot or bassinet, not on an adult bed, sofa or armchair.
- **B**ack: always place baby on their **back** for every sleep, day and night.
- **C**ot: a firm, flat mattress with a fitted sheet.

## Keep the sleep space empty
No pillows, loose blankets, soft toys, cot bumpers or wedges. A baby-safe sleep sack or a light fitted wrap is a safer way to keep baby warm.

## Room sharing
Experts generally recommend baby sleeps in the **same room** as parents, but on a separate surface, for at least the first 6 months.

## Don't overheat
Dress baby in one more layer than you would wear. If the neck or chest feels sweaty or hot, remove a layer. Keep the room at a comfortable temperature and don't cover the head.

## Other things that help
- Offer a pacifier at sleep time once feeding is going well, if baby takes it.
- Avoid smoke exposure around baby.
- Breastfeeding is linked with lower SIDS risk.
- Never leave baby to sleep in a car seat, swing or carrier outside of travel.

## Feeding at night
If you might fall asleep while feeding, prepare a safe set-up in advance rather than a sofa or soft bed. Put baby back in the cot as soon as you wake up.

## When to get help
If baby has trouble breathing, turns blue or pale, or is very hard to wake, get emergency medical help straight away.

*General information, not medical advice. Follow your paediatrician's guidance.*
