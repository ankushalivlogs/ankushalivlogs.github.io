# Baby Development Milestones: What's Normal by Month
Category: Babies
Description: A simple guide to baby milestones from 2 to 12 months, including smiling, rolling, sitting, crawling and first words, plus when to talk to your doctor.
Products: playing mat, play mat

Har baby apni raftaar se badhta hai. Ye guide ek rough idea deti hai, na ki race ka scoreboard.

## About 2 months
Social smiles, following faces and sounds, holding the head up briefly during tummy time, and cooing.

## About 4 months
Laughing, reaching for toys, bringing hands to mouth, holding the head steady, and pushing up on elbows during tummy time. Some babies start rolling.

## About 6 months
Rolling both ways, sitting with support, passing a toy from hand to hand, babbling, and responding to their own name. Time to start solids with your doctor's advice.

## About 9 months
Sitting without support, crawling or scooting, pulling to stand, saying "ba-ba" or "ma-ma", and playing peek-a-boo.

## About 12 months
Standing alone, cruising along furniture, maybe a first step, waving, pointing, and one or two simple words.

## How to help
- Daily tummy time and floor play
- Talk, sing and read to baby all day
- Offer safe toys that can be grasped and shaken
- Respond to babbling like a conversation

## Every baby is different
Premature babies follow their **adjusted** age. A range of a few months is normal for most milestones.

## When to talk to your doctor
Talk to your paediatrician if baby does not respond to sounds, doesn't follow things with the eyes, is very stiff or floppy, loses skills they had, or is not meeting several milestones for their age. Early help can make a real difference.

*General information, not medical advice. Track milestones with your paediatrician.*
