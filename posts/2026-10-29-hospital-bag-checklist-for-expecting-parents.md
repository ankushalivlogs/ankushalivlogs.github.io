# Hospital Bag Checklist for Expecting Parents
Category: Parents
Description: A simple hospital bag checklist for mom, baby and partner, with documents, clothes and comfort items to pack before delivery.

Delivery ki date paas aa rahi hai? Ye checklist 36th week tak pack kar lein, taki last minute mein bhaag-daud na ho.

## Documents (keep in one folder)
- ID proofs, insurance papers and hospital registration
- Antenatal reports and doctor's notes
- Cash and cards, plus a phone charger

## For mom
- 2 or 3 loose, front-open or nursing tops and nightwear
- Maternity pads and comfortable underwear
- Nursing bra and breast pads
- Slippers, socks and a light shawl
- Toiletries: toothbrush, soap, lip balm
- Hair ties, a water bottle and light snacks
- Going-home outfit that is loose and comfortable

## For baby
- 3 or 4 sets of clothes, soft and easy to open
- Newborn diapers and wipes
- Mittens, socks and cap
- 2 or 3 swaddles or soft blankets
- A car seat if you will travel by car, installed before you leave
- Going-home outfit

## For partner
- Change of clothes, charger, snacks and a small pillow
- A list of important phone numbers

## Nice to have
- Pen and notebook for questions
- Earphones or a playlist
- Camera or phone for first photos

## Before you go
Keep the bag by the door, and share its location with your partner or family. Save the hospital number and keep your fuel tank full. Ask the hospital what they provide, since many give baby items and pads.

*General checklist, not medical advice. Follow your doctor's and hospital's list.*
