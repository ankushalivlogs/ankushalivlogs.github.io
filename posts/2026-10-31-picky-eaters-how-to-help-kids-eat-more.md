# Picky Eaters: How to Help Kids Eat More Variety
Category: Kids
Description: Practical, pressure-free ways to help picky eaters accept new foods, from repeated exposure to making meals fun.

"Ye nahi khaunga!" Har ghar ki kahaani. Picky eating bahut common hai, aur usse jeetne ka tarika pressure nahi, patience hai.

## Why kids are picky
From around age 2, children naturally become wary of new foods. Appetite also slows down after infancy, so they may eat much less than before. This is normal.

## What works
1. **Offer again and again.** Many kids need 10 to 15 tries before accepting a new food. Don't give up after two.
2. **Serve small portions** of the new food next to a safe favourite.
3. **Eat together.** Kids copy what they see. Let them see you enjoying vegetables.
4. **Let them help.** Washing veggies, stirring or choosing from the market makes them curious.
5. **Make it fun.** Colourful plates, shapes, dips or a "rainbow day".
6. **Keep a routine.** Regular meals and snacks, with milk and juice limited so they come to the table hungry.

## What to avoid
- **Forcing, bribing or punishing.** It creates a bad relationship with food.
- **Using dessert as a reward** for eating vegetables.
- **Becoming a short-order cook,** making a separate meal for every refusal.
- **Distractions** such as TV or phone at meal time.

## Hide it? Only a little
Blending vegetables into sauce or parathas is fine as a bonus, but keep also serving them openly so kids learn to accept the real thing.

## When to talk to the doctor
If your child eats very few foods, loses weight, gags often, or has trouble chewing or swallowing, speak to your paediatrician.
