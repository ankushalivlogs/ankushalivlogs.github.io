# Baby Teething: Signs, Soothing Tips and What to Avoid
Category: Babies
Description: Teething signs, safe ways to soothe a teething baby, and what to avoid, including teething gels and amber necklaces.

Jab baby ka muh gila ho, wo har cheez chabaye aur raat ko chidchida ho, toh shayad daant aa rahe hain.

## When does teething start?
Most babies get their first tooth between 4 and 7 months, but anywhere from 3 to 12 months can be normal. The two bottom front teeth usually come first.

## Common signs
- Drooling more than usual and a drool rash on the chin
- Chewing on fingers, toys or anything nearby
- Sore, swollen or red gums
- Being fussy or sleeping badly
- Slightly raised temperature, but not a high fever

A high fever, diarrhoea or serious illness is **not** caused by teething. Please see your doctor.

## Safe ways to soothe
- **Clean finger massage:** gently rub baby's gums with a clean finger.
- **Cold, not frozen:** a chilled (not frozen) silicone teether or a cool, clean, damp cloth.
- **Wipe the drool** and apply a gentle barrier cream to prevent a rash.
- **Extra cuddles.** Comfort helps more than you think.

## What to avoid
- **Teething gels** with benzocaine or numbing medicines. Health regulators warn they can be dangerous for babies.
- **Amber necklaces or bracelets** because they are a choking and strangulation risk.
- **Hard or frozen items,** and anything small enough to choke on.
- Giving any medicine without asking your doctor first.

## Caring for first teeth
Once the first tooth shows up, brush twice a day with a soft baby brush and a rice-grain smear of fluoride toothpaste, as advised by your dentist or paediatrician. Plan the first dental visit by the first birthday.

*General parenting information, not medical advice.*
