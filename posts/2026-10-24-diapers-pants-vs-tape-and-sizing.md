# Diapers: pants or tape, and how to pick the right size
Products: mamy, diaper, pants

Diapers ek aisi cheez hain jo har parent din mein 6 se 10 baar use karta hai. Isliye thoda sa samajh lena bahut kaam aata hai.

## Pants vs tape
- **Tape diapers** suit newborns and young babies who mostly lie down. They are easy to adjust and sit below the umbilical cord stump.
- **Pull-up pants** suit babies who roll, crawl and wriggle. You change them like underwear, which is much easier when baby will not stay still. Many parents move to pants around the time baby starts rolling or sitting.

Neither is "better". Use what is easier for you and fits baby well.

## Getting the size right
Sizes are based on weight, and brands differ a little.
- **Too small:** red marks on the thighs or waist, hard to fasten, frequent leaks.
- **Too big:** gaps around the legs, sagging, leaks from the sides.

When baby is between sizes, a snug fit usually leaks less. Always check the weight range on the pack.

## How often to change
Every 2 to 3 hours for newborns, and straight away after a poo. Leaving a wet diaper on for long is the main cause of nappy rash.

## Quick checks that matter
- Newborns after the first week usually have around 6 or more wet diapers a day. Fewer can mean baby is not getting enough fluids; call your doctor.
- Clean from front to back, especially for girls.
- Pat dry, give a little air time, and use a barrier cream if skin looks red.
- If a rash does not improve in a few days, or has blisters or bleeding, see the doctor.

## Why a good absorbent diaper is worth it
A good fit with a soft inner layer keeps skin drier, gives longer sleep at night and means fewer outfit changes. For a growing baby, three or four sizes in a year add up, so buy a small pack first before stocking up.
