# Toddler Bedtime Routine That Actually Works
Category: Toddlers
Description: A simple, calming toddler bedtime routine, plus tips for handling bedtime resistance, night waking and nap changes.

Soney ka waqt aate hi toddler ki energy double ho jaati hai! Ek simple, same-every-night routine yahi ladai kam karti hai.

## Why a routine helps
Toddlers feel safe when they know what comes next. A predictable routine tells the brain that sleep is coming.

## A 30-minute routine
1. **Bath or a warm wash.** Calming and a clear start.
2. **Pyjamas and teeth.** Brush teeth before the last feed or snack.
3. **Dim the lights** and turn off screens at least an hour before bed.
4. **Story time.** One or two short books.
5. **Cuddles and a lullaby.**
6. **Lights out** at the same time every night.

## How much sleep?
Toddlers aged 1 to 2 typically need around 11 to 14 hours in 24 hours, including naps. Children aged 3 to 5 need about 10 to 13 hours.

## Common problems
- **"One more story!"** Set the number of books before you start, and stick to it.
- **Getting out of bed.** Calmly and quietly walk them back, with minimal talk.
- **Fear of the dark.** A dim night light and a favourite soft toy help.
- **Night waking.** Keep it short, boring and calm. No play or screens.
- **Giving up naps.** If nights get harder when the nap is late, move it earlier.

## Make it work
Keep weekends close to weekday timings, and stay consistent for at least a couple of weeks before judging if it works.

## When to get help
Loud snoring, pauses in breathing, frequent night terrors or severe sleep trouble that lasts should be discussed with your paediatrician.

*General parenting information, not medical advice.*
