# YouTube SEO guide for Ankushali Vlogs

For Ankur and Vaishali. Do this on every new upload (about 5 minutes). The website updates itself from what you write here.

## 1. Title (under 60 characters)
- Put the thing people search for FIRST, then the emotion, then the channel name last.
- Good: `Baby Walker Review: Is It Worth It? | Ankushali Vlogs`
- Avoid: long titles that get cut off, ALL CAPS, many emojis, and the channel name twice.
- Hindi videos: see section 6 for how to mix Hinglish and English.

## 2. Description (this is what also fills the website page)
The first two lines show in Google, so write them like a sentence a parent would search for.

```
In this vlog we show <what happens>. If you are a new parent looking for <topic>, this will help.
Is video mein: <same thing in Hinglish, the way you would type it in search>.

Products we used in this video:
Product name: https://link.amazon/xxxx
Another product: https://link.amazon/yyyy

Subscribe for new family vlogs every week: https://www.youtube.com/@Ankushali_Vlogs?sub_confirmation=1
Our website (product list + parenting articles): https://ankushalivlogs.github.io
Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/ankushali_
```

Rules that matter:
- One product per line, written as `Name: link`. The website reads exactly this and creates the product page for you.
- Use a clear product name (`Cerelac Baby Cereal`, not `link`).
- Never write `Ankushali` in a product name, the website treats that as a channel link and hides it.

## 3. Thumbnail
- Big face or baby close-up, 3 to 4 words of text maximum, bright colours.
- Same style across videos so people recognise the channel.

## 4. First 24 hours
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. It tells YouTube people are engaged.
- Pin one comment that asks a question (`What did your baby do at 6 months?`).
- Share the video to WhatsApp family groups and Instagram stories on day one.

## 5. Tags
Not important. Two or three relevant tags is enough. Title and description matter far more.

## 6. Hindi videos, Hinglish search
Your videos are in Hindi, but people search in three ways: Hinglish in English letters (`baby ko khana kaise khilaye`), English (`baby feeding tips`), and Hindi script. Cover the first two in every video. These rules are sensible guesses, not proven for this channel, so check them with step 5 below.

1. **Title:** start with the Hinglish phrase people type, then add the English term. Example: `Baby Ko Solid Food Kaise Shuru Karein | Baby Feeding Tips`. Keep it under 60 characters; drop the channel name if it does not fit.
2. **Description:** the first two lines in English (Google and the website both show these), then one Hinglish line, as in the template in section 2.
3. **Video language:** in YouTube Studio open the video > Details > Show more > Language, and set it to Hindi. This tells YouTube who to show it to.
4. **Captions:** YouTube writes Hindi captions by itself and often gets words wrong. In Studio > Subtitles, open the automatic Hindi captions, fix product names and key words, and publish. Do this at least for videos with products.
5. **Check what works:** change one video at a time. After 2 weeks, open YouTube Analytics > Reach > Traffic source: YouTube search, and compare with before. Keep what raises it.
