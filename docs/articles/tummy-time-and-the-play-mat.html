Tummy time and why a foldable play mat earns its space

Ek soft, saaf jagah jahan baby lete, palte aur khele, yahi play mat ka kaam hai. Lekin iska asli faayda tummy time hai.

What is tummy time?

Tummy time means placing baby on their tummy while awake and watched. It builds strength in the neck, shoulders and arms, which baby needs for rolling, sitting and crawling. It also helps prevent a flat spot at the back of the head.

How to do it

  • Start from the early days with 3 to 5 minutes at a time, a few times a day.
  • Slowly build up as baby gets stronger. Many guidelines suggest working towards around an hour in total across the day by about 3 months, in short sessions.
  • Do it on a firm, flat surface, never on a sofa or bed.
  • Get down on the floor, make eye contact, sing or place a mirror or toy in front.
  • If baby cries a lot, try after a nap, or lie baby on your chest first.

Safety: always supervise tummy time, and always put baby on their back to sleep.

Why a mat helps

  • Soft but firm. Cushioned enough for a bump, flat enough for safe play.
  • Clean surface. Wipes clean, so you can use it anywhere in the house.
  • Foldable. Folds up when guests come. Handy in small homes.
  • Bright prints keep babies interested for longer.

What to look for

  • Thickness of around 1 cm or more for comfort.
  • Non-toxic material, with no strong chemical smell.
  • Waterproof and easy to wipe.
  • No small detachable pieces baby can put in their mouth.
  • Non-slip bottom so it stays put on tiles.

Use it beyond tummy time

Sitting practice, reaching for toys, rolling, crawling and eventually first wobbly steps. One mat can serve for the first couple of years.
