Why parents choose anti-colic bottle nipples

Raat ke 2 baje jab baby ruk ruk ke rota hai aur aap samajh nahi paate kya karein, tab har parent koi bhi solution try karna chahta hai. Anti-colic nipples usually isi mod par kaam aate hain. Yahan hai ki ye kya hote hain aur kya nahi.

What is colic, really?

Doctors often describe colic as crying for more than 3 hours a day, on more than 3 days a week, for over 3 weeks, in a baby who is otherwise healthy and feeding well. It usually starts in the first few weeks, tends to peak around 6 weeks, and often settles by 3 to 4 months. Nobody knows the exact cause.

Why anti-colic nipples?

A regular nipple can let a baby swallow extra air along with milk. Anti-colic designs add a small vent or valve so air goes into the bottle instead of the baby's tummy. The idea is simple: less swallowed air, a little less gas and fussing during and after feeds.

To be honest, research on how much they help is mixed. Some babies settle noticeably, some show no change. That is why parents treat them as one small, low-cost thing to try, not a cure.

What to check before buying

  • Neck size. Nipples are made for a specific bottle neck, such as slim neck or wide neck. A wrong match leaks or collapses. Check this first.
  • Flow rate. Newborns need a slow flow. If milk pours too fast, baby gulps and chokes; too slow and baby gets tired and frustrated.
  • Material. Soft silicone is easy to clean and lasts longer than latex. Replace nipples when they look cracked, sticky or torn.

Things that help just as much

  • Feed with baby's head higher than the belly, bottle tilted so the nipple stays full of milk.
  • Pause to burp halfway and after the feed.
  • Try paced feeding, where baby takes breaks instead of drinking non-stop.
  • Sterilise bottles and nipples as your paediatrician advises.

When to call the doctor

Crying with fever, repeated vomiting, blood in the stool, poor weight gain or refusing feeds is not "just colic". Please get baby checked.
