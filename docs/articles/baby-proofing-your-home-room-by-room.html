Baby Proofing Your Home: A Room-by-Room Checklist

Share:WhatsAppCopy link

As soon as your baby starts rolling and crawling, your home becomes a new playground. A little preparation prevents a lot of trouble.

Start low

Get down on your knees and look around at baby's eye level. Anything you can reach, they will too.

Living room and bedroom

  • Anchor tall furniture, TVs and wardrobes to the wall so they cannot tip over.
  • Cover sharp corners with soft guards.
  • Use socket covers or safety plugs.
  • Tie up curtain and blind cords out of reach.
  • Keep small items, coins, buttons and batteries away. Button batteries are especially dangerous if swallowed.

Kitchen

  • Turn pot handles inwards and use back burners.
  • Keep knives, matches and cleaning liquids in high or locked cabinets.
  • Install a stove guard and cabinet locks.
  • Keep hot drinks away from table edges.

Bathroom

  • Never leave water in buckets or tubs.
  • Keep medicines, razors and cleaners locked away.
  • Use a non-slip mat and keep the toilet lid down.
  • Keep your water heater at a safe temperature.

Stairs, balconies and windows

  • Use safety gates at the top and bottom of stairs.
  • Fit window guards and balcony nets or railings baby cannot climb.
  • Keep furniture away from windows.

Everyday habits

  • Keep the poison helpline and emergency numbers on the fridge.
  • Learn basic infant CPR and first aid.
  • Remember that no gadget replaces supervision.

General safety information, not professional advice.
