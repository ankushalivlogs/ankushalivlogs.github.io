About

Gentle parenting, told like a family album.

Ankushali Vlogs is run by Ankur and Vaishali from Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, sharing real moments with their baby boy Kushagra. The content is calm, clean and useful, from daily baby-care routines to honest first-time-parent learnings. No noise, no drama.

“Our viewers are young Indian parents who are actively buying for their baby. They save our routines and message us for product links.”

Why brands choose us: baby care, diapers and wipes, feeding, nursery, mother and child, family lifestyle.

Good to know

Do you disclose paid partnerships?

Always. Sponsored posts are clearly marked, as required by Indian advertising rules.

Will you try a product before promoting it?

Yes. For baby products we check ingredients and age suitability first, and we only feature items that are safe for Kushu.

What do you not promote?

Anything unsafe for babies, medical claims, or products we have not used.

Can you share results?

Yes. After publishing we share views, saves and link clicks where the platform provides them.
